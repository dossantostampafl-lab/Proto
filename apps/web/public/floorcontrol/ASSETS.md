# Control-room images ("Controle" view)

Every file here is generated by `tools/render_control_room/render.py` (Blender/Cycles, modelled in
code, no third-party models, textures or photos). Licence: same as this repository.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `room.webp` | Landscape room render with anonymous unidentifiable operator silhouettes; no live labels, robots, text or numbers |
| `room-portrait.webp` | Portrait render focusing on the globe and command hub for phones |
| `globe.webp`, `globe-portrait.webp` | The globe alone through one turn (36 frames, transparent), played over the room |
| `robot.webp` | PROTO's own robot, four walk frames, transparent background |

Screens in the render show abstract glowing panels only. Operator silhouettes are static set dressing, not agents or proof of active work. Every value on the view (totals, agents,
status, queue, sources) is drawn live over the image by `src/views/floorControl/FloorControl.tsx`
from the API. Seat, hub and globe positions come from `src/views/floorControl/anchors.json`, which
the same script writes.

Coastlines on the globe and on the world-map screens come from Natural Earth 1:110m land
(`tools/render_control_room/ne_110m_land.geojson`, public domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com).

Models and lighting baked into the room come from Poly Haven, all CC0 (https://polyhaven.com/license),
downloaded by `tools/render_control_room/fetch_assets.py`: `potted_plant_01` (plants),
`modern_arm_chair_01` (desk chairs, recoloured), `sofa_02` (lounge) and the `blue_photo_studio`
HDRI (reflections only; the camera never sees it). Without them the renderer falls back to its
own primitives.
